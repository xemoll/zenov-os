ZenovOS 0.1.1 VM appliance acceptance markers

Required in the normal ISO boot log:
ZENOVOS_BOOT_OK
ZENOVFS_MOUNT_OK
GRAPHICAL_DESKTOP_READY
ZENOVOS_UI_READY

Required in the two-boot persistence log:
ZENOVOS_BOOT_OK (at least twice)
ISO_VM_PERSISTENCE_OK (write and replay)
SYNC_OK
ZENOVFS_FSCK_OK

Required host-side validation:
qemu-img check for qcow2, vdi and vmdk
16,777,216-byte virtual size for every data disk
round-trip conversion to raw and byte-for-byte comparison
SHA256SUMS.txt verification for all 14 direct assets
